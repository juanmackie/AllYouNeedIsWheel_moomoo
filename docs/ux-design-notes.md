# Dashboard UX design notes

These notes translate the cited UX guidance into review decisions for the one-screen, signals-only Moomoo dashboard. They guide presentation; ranking, freshness gates, and other domain rules remain defined by the application contracts.

## Design decisions

- **Keep the primary task and its status together.** Put connection/run state, coverage, freshness, and the top ranked signals where users encounter them before secondary analytics. This supports SBB's task-oriented and holistic principles: the signal is meaningful only alongside the operational context that makes it usable.
- **Use progressive disclosure for secondary detail.** Keep the shortlist legible at a glance, with risk metrics, candidate diagnostics, and analytics available on demand. Preserve warnings and source/freshness evidence in the visible path; reduced presentation means removing unnecessary detail, not hiding decision-critical context. (SBB: [Reduced](https://digital.sbb.ch/en/principles/ux-principles/overview/); [Self-explanatory](https://digital.sbb.ch/en/principles/ux-principles/overview/))
- **Name actions and states in the user's language.** Distinguish a signal from an order, explain why copy is unavailable, and give a next step for errors. Keep controls explicit ("Refresh run", "Copy ticket") and make read-only behavior understandable in context. (SBB: [Self-explanatory](https://digital.sbb.ch/en/principles/ux-principles/overview/); Vercel: [Content & Copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#content--copy))
- **Make state accessible without relying on color or pointer input.** Use semantic headings, buttons, links, labels, and tables; provide visible keyboard focus, accessible names for icon-only controls, and live announcements for asynchronous status. (SBB: [Inclusive](https://digital.sbb.ch/en/principles/ux-principles/overview/); Vercel: [Accessibility](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#accessibility), [Focus States](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#focus-states))
- **Format comparable values for quick scanning.** Align numeric columns and use tabular numerals; format dates, times, and currency consistently with locale-aware formatters. Keep the primary return measure distinct from supporting premium and risk values. (Vercel: [Typography](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#typography), [Locale & i18n](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#locale--i18n))
- **Keep interaction patterns consistent and robust.** Use buttons for actions and links for navigation, provide hover/active/focus feedback, support empty and long-content states, and ensure expanded sections remain usable on narrow screens. (SBB: [Recognisable](https://digital.sbb.ch/en/principles/ux-principles/overview/); Vercel: [Hover & Interactive States](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#hover--interactive-states), [Content Handling](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#content-handling), [Safe Areas & Layout](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md#safe-areas--layout))

## Sources

- SBB Digital, [Our design principles](https://digital.sbb.ch/en/principles/ux-principles/overview/) - principles are project-wide guidelines; overview describes appropriate, holistic, inclusive, recognisable, reduced, self-explanatory, task-oriented, and user-centred design.
- Vercel Labs, [Web Interface Guidelines (`command.md`)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/main/command.md) - practical review checklist for accessibility, focus, forms, typography, content handling, navigation, responsive layout, and interaction states. Treat it as a checklist, applying items that fit this server-rendered dashboard.
